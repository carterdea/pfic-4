Bottom Line

PFIC4 escalation is staged risk management for a TJP2 tight-junction/barrier disorder. Current approved treatments can reduce pruritus and enterohepatic bile-acid burden, but they do not repair TJP2.

PFIC4-specific evidence remains small. Maralixibat has direct randomized-trial inclusion of a small TJP2/PFIC4 subgroup in MARCH-PFIC; odevixibat has a PFIC indication and strong PFIC1/PFIC2 randomized evidence, with PFIC4 use relying more on broader PFIC/open-label/inference sources.

The practical escalation question is not only whether itch improves. It is whether itch/sleep, serum bile acids, bilirubin, AST/ALT/GGT, INR, albumin, platelets, growth, vitamins, imaging, elastography, and AFP/HCC surveillance are stable or improving.

IBAT Inhibitors

IBAT inhibitors enter the discussion when PFIC is suspected or confirmed and pruritus or serum bile-acid burden is clinically meaningful. For PFIC4, maralixibat has direct PFIC4 inclusion in MARCH-PFIC, but the subgroup was small. Odevixibat is PFIC-labeled, but PEDFIC 1 enrolled PFIC1/PFIC2 rather than PFIC4.

  • Both labels require attention to hepatotoxicity, diarrhea/dehydration, fat-soluble vitamin deficiency, fracture/bleeding concerns, and contraindication with prior or active hepatic decompensation events.
  • A symptomatic response should be paired with bile acids, bilirubin, growth, vitamins, and fibrosis/portal-hypertension markers before inferring disease control.

Nutrition, Diversion, And Transplant

Nutrition starts once cholestasis is recognized. Track growth, stool burden, vitamins A/D/E/K, INR/vitamin K status, bone health, and drug timing with sequestrants or IBAT inhibitors.

Biliary diversion or ileal exclusion enters after inadequate medical control, usually before advanced cirrhosis, through an expert PFIC/hepatobiliary surgery team. PFIC4-specific outcome data are limited.

Transplant enters when medical and surgical supportive strategies fail or liver risk dominates: end-stage disease, decompensation, HCC concern, refractory pruritus, growth failure, or refractory nutrition/vitamin burden.

HCC Surveillance

PFIC4 has published pediatric and adult HCC reports, but no reliable PFIC4-specific percentage risk can be assigned from current small cohorts. GeneReviews flags PFIC4 for HCC surveillance and recommends AFP plus abdominal ultrasound every 6-12 months in significant fibrosis or cirrhosis. The PFIC opinion paper recommends ultrasound and AFP every 6 months for established cirrhosis.

Unresolved Questions

  • What are the exact TJP2 variants, phase, predicted effect, and evidence for residual function?
  • What response threshold does the center use for PFIC4?
  • What interval is used for labs, vitamins, ultrasound, elastography, AFP, and HCC surveillance?
  • What is the explicit definition of inadequate IBAT response or intolerance?
  • Is biliary diversion appropriate for this genotype and fibrosis stage, or would transplant evaluation be safer?
