## Gene and disease-definition sources

### NCBI Gene: TJP2

URL: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/9414

Use in packet:

- Official gene symbol, full name, Gene ID, chromosome location, and protein-coding status.
- Function summary: tight-junction barrier component needed for tight-junction assembly.
- Alias and associated-phenotype context, including ZO2, PFIC4, and DFNA51.

### OMIM: TJP2 and PFIC4

TJP2 URL: https://omim.org/entry/607709

PFIC4 URL: https://omim.org/entry/615878

Use in packet:

- Gene-phenotype relationship between biallelic `TJP2` variants and PFIC4.
- Autosomal recessive inheritance framing.
- Mechanistic and clinical synopsis language for clinician-facing gene notes.

### Sambrotta et al., 2014

PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24614073/

PMC full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4061468/

Use in packet:

- Foundational report linking protein-truncating `TJP2` variants to progressive cholestatic liver disease.
- Evidence for absent TJP2 expression, claudin-1 mislocalization, tight-junction structural disruption, and severe pediatric disease.

### Tang et al., 2021

URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8421653/

Use in packet:

- Additional pathogenic `TJP2` variants and functional/mechanistic evidence.
- Cytoskeleton and cell-polarity context for clinician-facing pathophysiology.

### TJP2 deficiency and HCC reports

Zhou et al. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/hep.27872

Adult TJP2/PFIC4 family URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7015721/

## Drug labels

### Livmarli (maralixibat)

URL: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=64000394-1ef6-4e76-8ba8-11f25ba1b167

Use in packet:

- U.S. label indication for cholestatic pruritus in PFIC patients 12 months and older.
- PFIC dose/titration and formulation notes.
- Mechanism: reversible IBAT inhibition and reduced terminal ileum bile-acid reabsorption.
- Warnings: hepatotoxicity, GI adverse reactions, fat-soluble vitamin deficiency, fracture/bleeding, propylene glycol toxicity risk in children under 5 years using solution.
- Trial 2/NCT03905330 PFIC cohort: included PFIC4 patients and showed pruritus benefit vs placebo.

### Bylvay (odevixibat)

URL: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=151f1d3e-2bf4-47ce-b1be-a892de3258fa

Use in packet:

- U.S. label indication for pruritus in PFIC patients 3 months and older.
- PFIC dose/titration and administration.
- Mechanism: reversible IBAT inhibition and reduced terminal ileum bile-acid reabsorption.
- Warnings: hepatotoxicity, diarrhea/dehydration, fat-soluble vitamin deficiency, fracture/bleeding.
- Trial 1/NCT03566238 in PFIC1/PFIC2 and serum bile-acid/pruritus effects.

## Trials and trial registries

### MARCH-PFIC maralixibat trial

PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38723644/

ClinicalTrials.gov: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03905330

Use in packet:

- 26-week randomized placebo-controlled trial in pediatric PFIC.
- Included broader PFIC genotypes in the all-PFIC cohort, including TJP2/PFIC4.
- Supports maralixibat effect on pruritus and serum bile acids across the studied PFIC population.

### MARCH-ON maralixibat extension

ClinicalTrials.gov: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04185363

Public conference poster PDF: https://mirumpharma.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/R-Thompson-ESPGHAN-2024-Longterm-maintenance-of-response-after-2-years-of-MRX-treatment-in-PFIC_MARCHON-data.pdf

Use in packet:

- Open-label extension after MARCH-PFIC.
- Supports longer-term durability discussion for maralixibat.
- Lower evidentiary weight than blinded randomized data.

### PEDFIC odevixibat trial

PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35780807/

ClinicalTrials.gov: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03566238

Use in packet:

- 24-week randomized placebo-controlled trial in pediatric PFIC1/PFIC2.
- Supports odevixibat effect on pruritus and serum bile-acid response.
- Helps distinguish direct trial evidence from broader label indication and smaller non-PFIC1/2 evidence.

### PEDFIC 2 odevixibat extension

ClinicalTrials.gov: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03659916

EU trial results: https://www.clinicaltrialsregister.eu/ctr-search/trial/2017-002325-38/results

Use in packet:

- Open-label extension and broader PFIC enrollment.
- Supports longer-term and broader-genotype discussion for odevixibat.
- Not PFIC4-specific randomized evidence.

## Guidelines and expert reviews

### EASL Clinical Practice Guidelines on genetic cholestatic liver diseases

PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38851996/

Use in packet:

- Current expert guideline source for genetic cholestatic liver disease diagnosis, genetic testing, and pruritus management.

### GeneReviews: Pediatric Genetic Cholestatic Liver Disease Overview

URL: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK584020/

Use in packet:

- PFIC4/TJP2 clinical feature summary: low/normal GGT, severe cholestasis/pruritus, rapid progression risk, HCC concern, possible neurologic/respiratory deficits.
- Nutritional management: fat-soluble vitamins and medium-chain triglycerides.
- Pruritus management: UDCA, cholestyramine, rifampicin, IBAT inhibitors, naloxone/hydroxyzine/sertraline.
- Surgical options: partial external biliary diversion and partial ileal exclusion.
- Surveillance: fibrosis/cirrhosis, portal hypertension, HCC screening with ultrasound/AFP in significant fibrosis/cirrhosis; PFIC4 table flags HCC surveillance.

### Opinion paper on PFIC diagnosis and treatment

URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10772241/

## Main evidence limitations

- PFIC4-specific cohorts are very small.
- Maralixibat has randomized trial inclusion of PFIC4, but PFIC4 n was small.
- Odevixibat's pivotal randomized trial was PFIC1/PFIC2; broader PFIC approval and open-label/review evidence should not be overread as PFIC4-specific randomized evidence.
- Long-term prevention of fibrosis, liver failure, HCC, and transplant in PFIC4 remains uncertain.
